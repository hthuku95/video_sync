# Prospect Sources For Today's Sales Sprint

Use these sources to find SaaS/app/demo-pack prospects quickly. Prioritize public business/contact data only: website, company email, founder X handle, public LinkedIn, Product Hunt maker profile, launch-page links, and public bios.

## Highest-Priority Source Types

### 1. Recent Launch Directories

These are strong because buyers recently launched or are preparing to launch, which creates urgency.

- Product Hunt daily/new launches
- SuperPublic recent indie launches
- Indielyst startup listings
- Smol Launch weekly launches
- Better Launch monthly launch archive
- BetaList
- Uneed
- TinyLaunch
- Launching Next
- There's An AI For That
- Futurepedia

Best offer:

- SaaS/App Demo Pack
- Product Mockup Video Pack
- Thumbnail/Hero Visual Pack

Message angle:

> Your product is already launched; a short product video can help people understand it faster and give you better assets for X, Product Hunt, LinkedIn, and your homepage.

### 2. AI/SaaS Launches Without Strong Video

Search patterns:

- `site:producthunt.com/products "launching today" "AI"`
- `"launched today" "AI tool" "Product Hunt"`
- `"building in public" "launched" "SaaS"`
- `"Product Hunt" "founder" "demo video"`
- `"AI SaaS" "launch" "X"`

High-intent markers:

- Product has a good landing page but no demo video.
- Product is hard to explain from text alone.
- Founder posts actively on X.
- Product has a public roadmap, changelog, or launch announcement.

### 3. Agencies That Can Resell

Search patterns:

- `"SaaS marketing agency" founder`
- `"Webflow agency" "SaaS"`
- `"Framer agency" "launch"`
- `"AI automation agency" founder`
- `"short form content agency" founder`
- `"productized service" "SaaS"`

Best offer:

- Mixed Agency Production Bundle
- Agency Production Backend
- Product Mockup Video Pack

Message angle:

> This can help you add video/product visuals to client retainers without hiring a full video team.

### 4. Education / Technical Creators

Search patterns:

- `"course creator" "AI" "X"`
- `"math YouTuber" "course"`
- `"coding course" founder`
- `"finance educator" "YouTube"`
- `"technical explainer" "newsletter"`

Best offer:

- Education Explainer Pack
- Voice & Audio Production Pack
- Thumbnail/Hero Visual Pack

Message angle:

> Your topic would be easier to sell/teach with animated diagrams, narration, and reusable lesson visuals.

## Qualification Score

Score each prospect from 0-10:

- +2 has a live product URL
- +2 has public X handle
- +2 has public email/contact form
- +1 recently launched or announced update
- +1 landing page has no strong video/demo
- +1 product is visually explainable
- +1 likely has budget or sells B2B

Outreach order:

1. Score 8-10: send custom DM/email + offer sample today.
2. Score 6-7: send semi-custom DM/email.
3. Score 4-5: add to later follow-up.
4. Below 4: skip for today.

## Manual Outreach Rule

Do not spend more than 3 minutes enriching one cold prospect today. If no public X or email appears quickly, move on.

The target is speed plus relevance, not perfect CRM hygiene.
